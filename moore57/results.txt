=== reflection_bound.py ===
Reflection constructions: a counting impossibility

m = 2  (degree 3):  each branch needs 1 distinct labels in G \ 2G
    Z_2                          |G\2G| = 1    OK
    -> reflection construction POSSIBLE (best group supplies 1 of 1)

m = 6  (degree 7):  each branch needs 5 distinct labels in G \ 2G
    Z_2 x Z_3                    |G\2G| = 3    short by 2
    -> reflection construction IMPOSSIBLE (best group supplies 3 of 5)

m = 56  (degree 57):  each branch needs 55 distinct labels in G \ 2G
    Z_7 x Z_8                    |G\2G| = 28   short by 27
    Z_2 x Z_4 x Z_7              |G\2G| = 42   short by 13
    Z_2 x Z_2 x Z_2 x Z_7        |G\2G| = 49   short by 6
    -> reflection construction IMPOSSIBLE (best group supplies 49 of 55)

The verdict is exactly 'm is a power of two' in every case, which is
the theorem: G \ 2G has m - m/|G[2]| elements, and that reaches the
required m - 1 only when G[2] = G.

  degree     freedom          cost of conditions expected count
  7          10^18            10^-20             10^-2
  57         10^56886         10^-971757         10^-914871

  degree  7 : short by 2 orders of magnitude -- and the graph
              exists, so correlation bridges that easily.
  degree 57 : short by 914871 orders of magnitude.

That is the honest reading, and it cuts against the conjecture.  The
first-moment method is unreliable here -- it is wrong at degree 7 by
construction.  But being wrong by 2 orders and being wrong by
900,000 are not the same kind of claim.  The most likely conclusion
is that a degree-57 Moore graph would NOT have the involution
property: it is a genuine feature of m = 2 and m = 6 whose cost
grows far faster than the available freedom.

Consequences if so:
  * the gauged matching model is searching for something that does
    not exist, which explains why it stalls at four branches;
  * the earlier claim that the published t = 20 frontier is
    'off-path' was conditional on this conjecture, and should be
    withdrawn to that extent;
  * the cyclic ansatz is still dead, but on the independent grounds
    proved earlier (no order-56 automorphism; the reflection bound).
