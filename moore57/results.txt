Read the k=7 general row carefully: the first-moment crossover says
the structure should die at t=5 blocks, and the Hoffman-Singleton
graph nevertheless reaches t=7.  The estimate is wrong in the direction of
false pessimism, because the constraints are positively correlated.
So a search stalling near the crossover is NOT evidence of
non-existence -- at k=7 that same evidence would have been wrong.

==========================================================================
WHAT WOULD A BRUTE-FORCE SEARCH COST?
==========================================================================
  bijections to determine (after gauge)      1540, each from 56! = 10^74.9
  raw space                                  10^115272
  derangement constraints                    29260 triangles + 1185030 quadrilaterals = 1214290

  Raw space is the wrong measure -- pruning is what matters.  The
  size of the *pruned* tree is set by its widest level:

    search                 widest level   structures there tree size (nodes)
    k=7   general          t = 5          10^6             10^9
    k=57  cyclic ansatz    t = 22         10^183           10^185
    k=57  general          t = 20         10^5992          10^6067

    measured, k=7 general: 81,381,110 = 10^7.9 nodes to the first
    solution (61 s).  The estimate above says 10^9.1, so it runs
    about one order of magnitude high.  Treat the k=57 rows the same way.

  For scale: a machine visiting 10^18 nodes per second for the age
  of the universe covers 10^36 nodes.  Lloyd's bound on the total
  computation available in the observable universe is about 10^120.
  Even the cyclic-restricted exhaustive search exceeds that bound by
  65 orders of magnitude; the general search exceeds it by 5947.

  Symmetry does not rescue it.  The most any automorphism assumption
  can buy is the order of the assumed group, and that group is now
  known to have odd order (Ishida 2026) and order at most 375
  (Macaj-Siran 2010): a factor of 10^2.6.  The assumption is not even
  safe -- a trivial automorphism group is consistent with all of it.

  The honest measure of reach is the largest t-block structure anyone
  can build: t = 20 of 57.  Brute force would have to survive every
  block from there to 57.
