How accurate is this?

Tested against an existing die study: Schaefer's Roman Republican Die Project, Crawford RRC 393/1a. That is 676 coins whose dies were identified by hand. The thresholds were fixed in advance on a different issue (RRC 361), so this is a blind test.

At full scale

The same matcher was also run across Schaefer's whole die project. That covers 22,466 of his coins from 414 Crawford types, at most 200 coins per type, scored coin by coin. Here it uses the full-corpus pipeline, not the hand-tuned RRC 393 setup. The early rules chained borderline matches into a few giant false dies. The current rules, which this map uses, were set from a separate audit of the imperial coins before this test was run.

At full scale the current rules rarely join coins Schaefer separates, but they still miss about a third of the coins he puts on a shared die.

P (precision): of the coin pairs we put on one die, the share that Schaefer also puts on one die. R (recall): of the pairs Schaefer puts on one die, the share we found. ARI (adjusted Rand index): how closely our whole grouping into dies matches his, where 1 means identical.
