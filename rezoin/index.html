Newest release: Rezoin 1.0

November 22, 2026

  • Greek and Hellenistic coinage joins the Roman: 181,651 types in eleven catalogues, RIC, RPC and RRC, then Price, SC, PCO, AGCO, BIGR, LCO, Corpus Nummorum and IRIS.
  • A new model. On held-out Roman coins the right type is first 59% of the time and in the top ten 93%.
  • Choose a catalogue and Rezoin reads the coin only against that catalogue’s coinage.
  • Look a type up by its number (“RIC III 64”, “RRC 44/5”), and drag photos straight in.
  • Reference photos load from each catalogue’s website when you click; keeping a local copy is optional, in Settings.

Suggests catalogue types for photographs of ancient coins: Roman (RIC, RPC, RRC) and Greek and Hellenistic (Price, SC, PCO, AGCO, BIGR, LCO, Corpus Nummorum, IRIS). Version 1.0, for Mac and Windows.
