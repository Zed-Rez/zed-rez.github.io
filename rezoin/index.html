Reza Ramji

Rezoin

Suggests Roman Imperial (RIC) and Roman Provincial (RPC) types for photographs of ancient coins. Research preview, for Mac and Windows.

Latest version: see releases

Rezoin Lite

Download

Drag in a coin photo, the two photos of one coin, or a folder of coins. Reference photos load from OCRE and RPC online on a click. Mac and Windows in one zip, with test coins and a one-page guide.

Rezoin Princeton

Download

The full edition: catalogue search, export to spreadsheet and labels, test coins and example outputs. Mac and Windows in one zip. Its reference photos are separate downloads, below; without them it loads photos from OCRE and RPC online on a click, as Lite does.

Reference photos for Rezoin Princeton

One zip per catalogue volume: take only the volumes you need. Unzip each and move the folder it makes (Rezoin photos RIC IV, …) anywhere into the Rezoin Princeton folder; Rezoin finds it the next time it opens.

The volumes are listed on GitHub.

Opening it

Mac: unzip, then double-click the app. If macOS will not open it, Control-click it → Open, or System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway. Windows: unzip, then double-click the .exe in the Windows folder. At “Windows protected your PC”, click More info → Run anyway.
