StartupGPT

A 4,512-parameter GPT that names startups, running entirely in your browser.

This is Karpathy's microGPT — a complete GPT in 200 lines of dependency-free Python — retrained on 24,614 verified startup names gathered from accelerator portfolios (Y Combinator, Techstars, 500 Global, SOSV, Antler, Plug and Play, Creative Destruction Lab, and friends) and recent SEC Form D filings. Generic trailing words like Labs, Technologies, AI, and Inc were stripped before training so the model learns the name, not the paperwork.

Every generated name is cross-checked against an index of 27,999 real startup names: it must be new (not in the index) yet plausible (within an edit distance of 2 of something real). The model keeps rolling until a name passes.

Character-level transformer: 1 layer, 4 heads, 16-dim embeddings, 4,512 parameters, trained for 1,000 steps of pure-Python Adam. Inference and the cross-check both run locally on this page; nothing is sent anywhere.
